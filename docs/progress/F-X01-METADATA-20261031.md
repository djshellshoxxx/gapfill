# Harmonic recipe sidecar codec —2026-10-31

`harmonic::psola::DecisionMetadata` is12 bytes, separate from FillRecipe's frozen
16 bytes/all eight existing flags. `Metadata.h/.cpp` owns validation and exact
12-byte little-endian encode/decode (no allocations, no JUCE or persistence IO).

Bytes0..3 are version/outcome/intervals/voices; bytes4..7 are source f0Hz and
8..11 clarity, both IEEE float32 little endian. Absent version0 requires all-zero
fields, including float sign bits. Version1 uses interval enum0..5, voices1..4.
Used1 and EpochFallback3 require finite50..1000Hz and clarity0.8..1;
UnvoicedFallback2 requires f0Hz0 and finite clarity0..1. Other outcomes/versions,
non-finite values and wrong payload lengths are rejected. Decode failure clears
the optional metadata; the outer codec preserves the independently validated
base recipe. Encode failure leaves its destination untouched.

The final voice decides Used versus EpochFallback, not merely the earlier pitch
query. New-mode unvoiced decisions record UnvoicedFallback. Missing metadata
preserves legacy replay. Seed comes from the existing recorded32-bit recipe seed;
other creative controls keep their established current-settings policy.

Three strict standalone tests pass exact byte-layout/round-trip checks, unknown
version/outcome/truncation/NaN/negative-zero rejection and atomic failed encoding.
Shared RecipeExtras/event/history/Lock/template envelopes and audio hooks are
owned by the coordinator/standards worker; this commit supplies their pure codec.
